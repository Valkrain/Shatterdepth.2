# [Working Title]: Narrative & Lore Bible

## 1. High-Concept & Tone
* **Genre:** 2D Side-Scrolling Pixel-Destruction Roguelite
* **Aesthetic:** Arcane-Industrial / Magitek / Ruined High-Fantasy
* **Core Tone:** Chaotic, scrappy, high-velocity, darkly humorous, and volatile. Everything is unstable—the terrain, the guns, and the physics.
* **The Hook:** Deep beneath a shattered world lies a 30-tier subterranean labyrinth of flooded catacombs, runaway foundries, and acid-soaked crystal vaults. Armed with wildly unstable arcane-industrial weaponry, you descend from the **Subterranean Bastion** as an indebted Penal Delver to blast apart modular, self-assembling Colossi and carve your own chaotic path through the destructible earth.

## 2. Deep History: The Builders, The Break & The Debt

### Era I: The Aether-Wrights of Vael & The Thirty-Tier Descent (The Builders)
* **Who Built It:** Millennia ago, an enigmatic order of silent star-gazers and geomancers known only in fragmented inscriptions as **The Aether-Wrights of Vael** (or simply *The Veiled Choir*) turned their backs on the sun and carved an empire into the living stone. Unlike surface kings who built with mortal sweat, the Aether-Wrights bound celestial harmonics into living brass, stone, and crystal.
* **The Thirty-Tier Tri-Strata Design:** To commune with and harvest the planet's sleeping heart without staining their own robes with soot, they hollowed out the continent into **three sequential subterranean strata—each carved ten levels deep** (30 depths total):
  1. **First Stratum — Sunken Masonry (Levels 1–10):** The upper ten tiers of sacred aqueducts, limestone sanctuaries, and astronomical archive vaults, engineered to channel subterranean rivers into coolant for the engines below.
  2. **Second Stratum — Ashen Foundry (Levels 11–20):** Beneath the tenth water-lock, ten industrial tiers of basalt smelteries and oil-fired forges tapped the planet's magma veins to smelt star-metal alloys and shape tireless brass-and-stone servants.
  3. **Third Stratum — Vitriol Vaults (Levels 21–30):** Below the lowest slag-gates lay the final ten extraction tiers, where caustic alchemical waters (vitriol) were used to weep away the deep bedrock and expose raw, singing crystal cores (*Sapphire, Furnace-Heart, and Astral Nuclei*).
* **The Golden Covenant:** To the Aether-Wrights, gold was not mere coin—it was **frozen sunlight**, the only earthly metal capable of holding an astral soul-spark without burning away. They wove an unbreakable taboo into every construct's mind-stone: **Gold is inviolate.** Every smelter, acid vat, and guardian automaton across all thirty levels was bound to protect, refine, and hoard gold above all else.

### Era II: The Great Resonance Collapse (What Went Wrong)
* **The Hubris at Level 30:** Seeking to awaken the sleeping heart of the deep earth at the bottom of the **Thirtieth Vault**, the Aether-Wrights bored past the final boundary of the Vitriol Vaults. At Level 30, their harmonic drills struck a primal fault, triggering a cataclysmic backlash remembered only as **The Fracture**.
* **The Cascading Thirty-Level Ruin:** The shockwave did not strike all at once—it tore upward through all thirty tiers in three catastrophic waves:
  * *First (Levels 30 down to 21):* Boiling acid reservoirs ruptured across the ten tiers of the **Vitriol Vaults**, dissolving the Aether-Wrights' inner sanctums and unshackling wild prism-energy into the caverns.
  * *Second (Levels 20 down to 11):* The seismic tremor cracked the magma ducts and oil cisterns of the **Ashen Foundry**, flooding all ten smelting tiers with superheated slag, soot, and runaway kiln-fire.
  * *Third (Levels 10 down to 1):* Reaching the upper ten tiers of the **Sunken Masonry**, the quake shattered the ancient aqueducts, drowning the archive halls and turning the limestone vaults into a labyrinth of flooded, collapsing rubble.
* **The Runaway Engine:** By the time the tremor reached the surface, every Aether-Wright had been dissolved into shadow and ash, **but their bound constructs never received a release from their vows.** Trapped in a broken, looping liturgy of maintenance and defense, the deep Foundry Core interpreted the thirty-tier collapse as a "profane invasion of the vaults."
  * *Crawlers* and *Drones* shifted from silent custodians to frantic, aggressive scavengers.
  * Elemental hazards (*Slimes, Pyro Imps, Crystal Wisps*) bled out of ruptured acid vats, runaway kilns, and cracked geodes—and were immediately recognized by the ancient wards as "sanctified guardians."
  * Worse, the blind assembly lines began scavenging fallen temple pillars, artillery barrels, and raw crystals to bolt together towering **Modular Colossi**—stationing a supreme war-idol at the tenth floor of each stratum (*Level 10, Level 20, and Level 30*) to seal the gates against thieves.

### Era III: The Crown Exchequery & The Penal Bastion (The Present)
* **The Surface Regime:** Centuries later, the kingdoms above—battered by endless wars and starving for coin—discovered the sunken stairways leading into Vael's ruins. Rather than send noble knights to die in acid-flooded tunnels, the royal treasury—known as **The Crown Exchequery** (enforced by its iron-fisted **Bailiff-Wardens**)—claimed all thirty levels of the ruins as a royal debt-pit.
* **Why Send Prisoners?** Royal soldiers demand pensions; convicts cost nothing. The Exchequery hammered an iron garrison—**The Subterranean Bastion**—directly into the upper cavern mouth above Level 1 to tax everything coming up from the dark.
* **The Sentence:** Tax-evaders, deserters, bankrupt merchants, hedge-wizards, and common cutpurses have their debts tallied in brass ledgers, are handed volatile, black-powder and arcane-rigged mining guns, and are kicked into the **Expedition Descent Portal**. Every ounce of sacred Vaelic gold hauled back up shaves a mark off their blood-debt—and only those who survive all thirty floors earn full clemency.

## 3. The Subterranean Bastion (Hub World Lore)
Wedged into the upper bedrock where the Crown Exchequery's ironworks meet the ancient stonework of Vael:
* **West Archive Alcove:** Home to **The Archivist (Keeper of Fractured Annals)**. Nobody knows if The Archivist is a surviving Vaelic construct draped in moth-eaten robes, an immortal royal clerk serving out a treason sentence, or a ghost bound to the ledger-ink—only that they obsessively record every gun schematic, colossus part, and delver death in the *Archive Gallery*.
* **Central Foundry Hearth:** The Bastion's beating heart, featuring the molten grate walkway, the *Class/Vault Pedestal* (where delvers are outfitted with specialized penal rigs), and the *Controls/Settings Pedestal*.
* **Upper Target Range Deck:** Outfitted with four auto-respawning test dummies (*Light, High Perch, Armored, and Heavy Plating*) bolted together from salvaged Sentinel scrap so convicts can learn which guns ricochet before dying downstairs.
* **East Expedition Staging Dais:** Houses the *Loadout Staging Pedestal* and the *Expedition Descent Portal*—a captured Vaelic way-gate that flings delvers into Level 1 and yanks their charred remains (or soul-imprint) back up when a run ends.

## 4. The 3 Run Biomes (30-Level Progression)
1. **Stratum I — Sunken Masonry (Levels 1–10)**
   * *Depth Progression:* Tidal Cavern (1–3) → Deep Slate Grotto (4–6) → Abyssal Masonry (7–9) → Hadal Trench (10)
   * *Lore:* The first ten tiers of drowned sanctuaries and ruptured cooling aqueducts. Patrolled by **Stone Wardens**—ancient temple idols retrofitted by the Runaway Engine into heavy masonry juggernauts.
   * *Level 10 Gatekeeper Boss:* **Monolith Warden** (Weak Spot: *Sapphire Core*) — The prime hydraulic gatekeeper guarding the sluice-stairs down to the Foundry.
2. **Stratum II — Ashen Foundry (Levels 11–20)**
   * *Depth Progression:* Smoldering Forge (11–13) → Cinder Smeltery (14–16) → Magma Ducts (17–19) → Crucible Core (20)
   * *Lore:* Ten descending tiers of automated smelteries and oil-fired assembly lines still churning out star-metal plates and ordnance centuries after their mystic masters turned to ash.
   * *Level 20 Gatekeeper Boss:* **Slag Colossus** (Weak Spot: *Furnace Heart*) — A walking blast-furnace assembled directly on the deepest smelter floor.
3. **Stratum III — Vitriol Vaults (Levels 21–30)**
   * *Depth Progression:* Jade Fracture (21–23) → Prismatic Vault (24–26) → Amethyst Fault (27–29) → Astral Geode (30)
   * *Lore:* The final ten tiers of harmonic crystal mines where caustic acid baths stripped away the earth to expose raw astral geodes—and where the Fracture began at Level 30.
   * *Level 30 Final Boss:* **Sovereign Spire** (Weak Spot: *Crystal Nucleus*) — A hovering, geometric prism-engine pulsing with the same runaway frequency that destroyed Vael.

## 5. Why the Environment is Destructible (In-Lore)
* Centuries of runaway mining, acid corrosion, thermal expansion, and sonic crystal resonance have rotted all thirty subterranean tiers from the inside out. Limestone is hollow, mortar is brittle, and pressurized oil pipes run right behind load-bearing walls. Every firefight is a structural disaster waiting to happen.
