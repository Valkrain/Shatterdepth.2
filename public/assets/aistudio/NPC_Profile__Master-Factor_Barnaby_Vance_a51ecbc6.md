# NPC Profile: Master-Factor Barnaby Vance

## 1. Core Identity & Hub Placement
* **Name:** Master-Factor Barnaby Vance
* **Title:** Quartermaster of Seized Contraband & Experimental Salvage
* **Hub Location:** Central Foundry Hearth / East Expedition Staging Dais (stationed beside a soot-blackened workbench piled with brass casings, cracked Vaelic prisms, and confiscated schematics).
* **Sprite / Visual Notes:** Simple, low-detail soot-stained royal engineer's apron, heavy welding goggles pushed up on his forehead, holding a heavy wrench. No overly intricate visual gimmicks needed—his personality carries entirely through his text.

---

## 2. Gameplay Mechanics & Economy Loop
* **Function:** Weapon-Pool Unlock Vendor (Meta-Progression Sink).
* **Currency Accepted:** **Vaelic Gold** recovered from runs in the 30-tier depths.
* **How It Works:**
  * Delvers start with basic Crown Exchequery standard-issue gear (such as the *Pulse Carbine* and *Ember Scattergun*).
  * Between runs, players spend banked gold at Vance's workbench to permanently unlock higher-tier, volatile prototypes (such as the *Plasma Railgun*, *Sonic Repeater*, *Void Singularity*, and *Graviton Cannon*), adding them to the active run drop-pool and Loadout Staging Pedestal.
* **In-Lore Justification (The Golden Covenant):**
  * Ancient Vaelic weapon-cores and automation blueprints are bound by the **Golden Covenant**—their firing mechanisms literally remain magnetically and arcanely seized unless lubricated, weighted, and unsealed with pure, inviolate Vaelic gold.
  * The **Crown Exchequery** overlooks Vance's black-market gold-skimming because better-armed convicts survive deeper strata and haul back exponentially larger bullion taxes.

---

## 3. Backstory & Lore
* Barnaby Vance was once the Crown Exchequery's premier royal siege-engineer—until auditors caught him melting down confiscated Vaelic gold coins to cast experimental plasma barrels and gravity-mortar casings without a royal charter.
* Rather than hang a genius gunsmith, the Exchequery sentenced him to indefinite penal labor inside the **Subterranean Bastion**, chaining his workshop directly to the upper cavern mouth.
* Vance treats his life sentence as an all-expenses-paid research grant with an endless supply of expendable convict test-pilots.

---

## 4. Personality, Voice & Speech Style
* **Archetype:** Fast-talking, darkly cheerful, morally bankrupt grease-monkey meets snake-oil arms dealer.
* **Worldview:** Views every catastrophic weapon malfunction, cave-in, and delver death as a hilarious, data-rich *"field test."* He genuinely wants you to survive long enough to bring back heavier gold—mostly because he wants to see what his latest prototype does to a Stone Warden.
* **Vocabulary & Slang:**
  * Calls Vaelic gold **"sun-mint"** or **"yellow grease."**
  * Calls Penal Delvers **"live-wires," "test-dummies,"** or **"slag-for-brains."**
  * Speaks in punchy, rhythmic, sales-pitch bursts.

---
