## Part 2: The 10 Damage Types (UI Hover Tooltips)

1. **Kinetic (`KINETIC`):** Shreds soft earth, wood, and jars, but deflects off metal and stone.
2. **Explosive (`EXPLOSIVE`):** Blasts apart brick, stone, and crystal, though heavy metal absorbs the shock.
3. **Thermal (`THERMAL`):** Incinerates wood, moss, and oil vats, but barely scorches stone or steel.
4. **Plasma (`PLASMA`):** Superheated rounds that melt straight through metal, stone, and brick alike.
5. **Arc (`ARC`):** Shatters crystals and conducts through metal and water; insulated by dirt and wood.
6. **Corrosive (`CORROSIVE`):** Dissolves metal plating and mortar, while gold and crystal remain unharmed.
7. **Laser (`LASER`):** Cuts cleanly through stone, crystal, and gold, though steel partially deflects it.
8. **Cryogenic (`CRYOGENIC`):** Flash-freezes enemies, makes stone brittle, and solidifies molten lava.
9. **Sonic (`SONIC`):** Structural vibrations that rattle brick and stone foundations apart.
10. **Void (`VOID`):** Gravitational collapse that tears terrain and enemies into a central point.
