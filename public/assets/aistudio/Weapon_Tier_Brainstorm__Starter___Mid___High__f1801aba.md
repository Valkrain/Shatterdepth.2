# Weapon Tier Brainstorm: Starter, Mid & High Tier Concepts

*Designed to fit seamlessly into the 10-damage-type system, pixel-destruction physics, and the lore of the Crown Exchequery, Master-Factor Barnaby Vance, and the Aether-Wrights of Vael.*

---

## Tier 1: Starter Weapons (Crown Exchequery Penal Surplus)
*Crude, mass-produced mining and riot tools issued to fresh convicts at zero cost. Reliable for basic excavation, but saddled with glaring material blind spots.*

### 1. Rivet Puncher (`RIVET_PUNCHER`)
* **Damage Type:** Kinetic / Explosive (Delayed)
* **Concept:** Fires heavy, superheated iron construction rivets at high velocity. Unlike standard bullets that bounce off stone, rivets embed themselves into brick, stone, and armored enemies before popping with a small concussive charge a half-second later.
* **Lore:** Re-purposed pneumatic bridge-building tools from the Exchequery docks.
* **Flavor Text:** 
  > *"Standard-issue for stubborn masonry and even more stubborn union organizers."*

### 2. Cinder Bellows (`CINDER_BELLOWS`)
* **Damage Type:** Thermal
* **Concept:** A short-range, wide-cone coal-dust blower that spews lingering sparks and burning embers. Terrible range and useless against stone, but instantly ignites oil vats, moss, wooden bridges, and entire packs of Crawlers.
* **Lore:** A rusted foundry furnace-igniter fitted with a leather bellows pump.
* **Flavor Text:** 
  > *"Keep your eyebrows trimmed and your back to the wind."*

### 3. Brine Sprayer (`BRINE_SPRAYER`)
* **Damage Type:** Corrosive (Light) + Conductive Primer
* **Concept:** Pumps pressurized, salt-and-vitriol bilge water in a heavy arc. Deals light corrosion to metal and leaves wet puddles that dramatically amplify **Arc** damage if you swap weapons or shoot near crystal circuits.
* **Lore:** Salvaged from the flood-pumps in the upper Sunken Masonry.
* **Flavor Text:** 
  > *"Smells like low tide and dissolves Exchequery boot-leather in forty seconds flat."*

### 4. Quarry Thumper (`QUARRY_THUMPER`)
* **Damage Type:** Kinetic (Heavy Impact)
* **Concept:** A single-shot, break-action demolition cannon that lobs heavy iron slag-balls. Low fire rate and heavy gravity drop, but smashes cleanly through brick walls and knocks Stone Wardens backward.
* **Lore:** Built to clear collapsed coal-shafts; mostly used to clear hallway bottlenecks.
* **Flavor Text:** 
  > *"Technically a mining tool, assuming the ore you're mining is made of Sentinel ribs."*

## Tier 2: Mid-Tier Weapons (Barnaby Vance's Contraband Prototypes)
*Unstable, jury-rigged hybrids bolted together by Master-Factor Barnaby Vance out of salvaged Vaelic components and royal surplus. Unlocked with moderate gold.*

### 5. Slag Slinger (`SLAG_SLINGER`)
* **Damage Type:** Thermal + Liquid Spawn
* **Concept:** Lobs gloopy blobs of molten Ashen Foundry slag that stick to walls and ceilings, burning through wood and dripping temporary pools of cooling lava onto enemies below.
* **Lore:** Vance tapped a pressurized smelter pipe directly into a mortar tube and slapped a wooden handle on it.
* **Flavor Text:** 
  > *"Vance's Third Law: Anything becomes a liquid if you hate it hot enough."*

### 6. Aether Sawblade (`AETHER_SAW`)
* **Damage Type:** Laser / Kinetic
* **Concept:** Launches spinning, laser-rimmed Vaelic survey discs that slice through soft earth and wood while ricocheting violently off heavy metal and stone corridors up to 4 times.
* **Lore:** Built from stripped-down Drone gyroscopes wired to a cracked mining crystal.
* **Flavor Text:** 
  > *"Bouncing blades don't have friends, so duck after pulling the trigger."*

### 7. Rime Harpoon (`RIME_HARPOON`)
* **Damage Type:** Cryogenic
* **Concept:** Fires a tethered cryogenic spike that freezes the first target or terrain chunk it hits, making stone brittle enough to shatter with a single follow-up shot—or yanking frozen enemies toward you.
* **Lore:** Originally engineered to cool runaway magma vents in Stratum II before Vance added a winch.
* **Flavor Text:** 
  > *"Turns a charging Stone Warden into a very large, very fragile ice sculpture."*

### 8. Resonance Fork (`RESONANCE_FORK`)
* **Damage Type:** Sonic
* **Concept:** A two-pronged Vaelic tuning-fork rifle that fires a slow-moving harmonic wave. Passing through brick or stone causes the blocks to vibrate and crumble behind the wave, triggering delayed chain-reaction collapses.
* **Lore:** Stolen from an Aether-Wright observatory altar and crudely wired to a hand-crank battery.
* **Flavor Text:** 
  > *"Hum a bar, shatter a load-bearing pillar, bury a squad of Sentinels."*

## Tier 3: High-Tier Weapons (Forbidden Vaelic Relics)
*True, unshackled instruments of the Aether-Wrights of Vael, sealed behind heavy Golden Covenant locks at Vance's workbench. Devastating terrain-erasers.*

### 9. Sun-Siphon Lance (`SUN_SIPHON_LANCE`)
* **Damage Type:** Plasma / Laser
* **Concept:** Channels a searing beam of "frozen sunlight." The beam's width, blast power, and armor-melt speed scale dynamically with how much unspent **Gold** you are carrying on the current run.
* **Lore:** A sacred Vaelic ceremonial scepter that draws power directly from the Golden Covenant itself.
* **Flavor Text:** 
  > *"Proof that enough gold really can vaporize all your problems."*

### 10. Oblivion Chime (`OBLIVION_CHIME`)
* **Damage Type:** Void
* **Concept:** Strikes a miniature dark-crystal bell, firing a silent wave that hangs in mid-air before imploding—cleanly erasing a circular chunk of all non-gold terrain (even heavy metal) into nothingness.
* **Lore:** Used by the Veiled Choir to carve the thirty subterranean tiers without leaving rubble behind.
* **Flavor Text:** 
  > *"Where did that wall go? Better question: where did the guy standing next to it go?"*

### 11. Tempest Choir (`TEMPEST_CHOIR`)
* **Damage Type:** Arc
* **Concept:** Fires a slow-drifting ball-lightning orb that continuously arcs lethal lightning tendrils to nearby crystals, metal plates, water pools, and enemies as it floats across the room.
* **Lore:** A caged storm-spirit harvested from the deepest lightning-rods of the Astral Geode.
* **Flavor Text:** 
  > *"Turns flooded tunnels into an all-you-can-fry buffet."*

### 12. Tectonic Ram (`TECTONIC_RAM`)
* **Damage Type:** Explosive / Sonic
* **Concept:** A massive, shoulder-mounted Vaelic pile-driver that unleashes a directional seismic shockwave—blasting a horizontal tunnel straight through stone and brick while sending lethal rock-shrapnel flying forward.
* **Lore:** The heavy boring-engine that caused the Great Resonance Collapse at Level 30, miniaturized for handheld use.
* **Flavor Text:** 
  > *"Why look for a doorway when you have four rounds left in the chamber?"*
