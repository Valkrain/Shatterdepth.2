Severing a destructible part (Crown, Left Limb, Right Limb, Base) disables its special attacks.

   - DOME (Prism Dome / Prefix: "Vaulted") -> Special: Orbital Prism Dome (CROWN_DOME)
   - PRONG_SPIRE (Rail Spire / Prefix: "Spire-Crowned") -> Special: Apex Rail Lance (CROWN_PRONG_SPIRE)
